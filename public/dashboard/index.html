      Tools

      Score it, then price it

      Both tools save to the client selected above, so the numbers are there when you write the Audit Report and Quotation.

      Opportunity Matrix

      Score each opportunity 1–5. Difficulty 5 is hardest. Score = Impact × (6 − Difficulty) × Confidence, out of 125.

      ROI & Price Calculator

      Price from value, not hours. A starting point is 10–20% of the first-year value, so the client sees a 5–10× return.
