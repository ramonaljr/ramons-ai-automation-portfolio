# Fernhill Outdoor Supply: Store Policies and Product FAQ

Fernhill Outdoor Supply is a fictional sample client used in Ramon Vallejera's
portfolio. This document is the support agent's knowledge base: it answers
from this text and never makes up a policy that is not written here.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

## About us

Fernhill Outdoor Supply is a small online outdoor gear store based in
Portland, Oregon. We sell tents, sleeping gear, packs, footwear, clothing,
camp cooking gear and accessories. We ship to the United States and Canada.

Customer support hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00 Pacific Time.
The AI assistant answers at any time; a person picks up anything it hands
over on the next working day.

## Shipping

- **Processing time:** orders ship within 1 to 2 working days.
- **United States:** standard shipping takes 3 to 5 working days after
  dispatch. Shipping is free on orders of $100 or more; otherwise it costs
  $9.95. Carriers: UPS, USPS or FedEx, chosen by us.
- **Canada:** shipping takes 5 to 10 working days with Canada Post. Orders
  can be held at customs; customs duties, if any, are paid by the customer.
- **Tracking:** a tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking
  can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.
- **Delays:** if an order is more than 5 working days past its estimated
  delivery date, we open a trace with the carrier. If the carrier confirms
  the parcel is lost, we offer a free replacement or a full refund (a team
  member approves either one).
- **Changing the address:** possible only while the order is still
  Processing. Once shipped, the address cannot be changed.
- **We cannot ship:** fuel canisters, because they are hazardous goods.

## Returns

- **Return window:** 30 days from the delivery date.
- **Condition:** items must be unused, with tags, in the original packaging.
  Footwear must be unworn outdoors.
- **How to return:** contact us with the order number. We email a prepaid
  return label for US orders. Canadian customers pay return shipping.
- **Return shipping:** free for US orders; $12 is deducted from the refund
  if the customer uses our label for a change of mind (not for faulty or
  wrong items).
- **Outside 30 days:** we cannot accept change-of-mind returns. Faulty items
  are covered by the warranty below.
- **Final sale items:** none at the moment.

## Exchanges

We exchange sizes and colours free of charge within 30 days of delivery,
subject to stock. The customer sends the original item back with our label,
and we ship the new one when the return is scanned by the carrier.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days
  after we receive and inspect the return.
- Cancelled orders that had not shipped are refunded in full straight away.
- **Every refund, partial refund, replacement or discount is approved by a
  team member before it is issued.** The assistant can start the request
  but cannot promise the outcome.

## Wrong, damaged or faulty items

- If an item arrives damaged or we sent the wrong item, contact us within
  14 days of delivery with the order number and a photo.
- We send the correct or replacement item at no cost, or refund it in full,
  and we cover all shipping. A team member approves it.
- **Warranty:** all Fernhill gear has a 1-year warranty against
  manufacturing defects (seams, zips, buckles, valves). Normal wear and
  accidental damage are not covered.

## Order changes and cancellations

- Orders can be cancelled or changed while they are still Processing.
- Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled; the customer can return
  it under the returns policy.
- Backordered items ship as soon as stock arrives; the customer can cancel
  a backordered item at any time before it ships for a full refund.

## Payment and pricing

- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Shop Pay.
- Prices are in US dollars. Canadian orders are charged in US dollars.
- **Price adjustments:** if an item goes on sale within 7 days of delivery,
  we refund the difference once (approved by a team member).
- Discount codes cannot be combined, and cannot be added after an order is
  placed.

## Privacy

We never ask for card numbers, passwords or full payment details in chat,
WhatsApp or email. The assistant only needs the order number and the email
address used for the order.

## When the assistant hands over to a person

The assistant passes the conversation to a team member when a customer is
angry or upset, mentions a lawyer, chargeback or legal action, reports an
injury or safety problem, asks for something this document does not cover,
or contacts us a third time about the same problem.
