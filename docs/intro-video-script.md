# Intro Video Script — AI Automation Specialist

**Format:** talking head, one camera · **Length:** ~80 seconds (~200 words at a relaxed pace)
**Use:** Upwork profile video and landing page (same file). There are no URLs, emails or
other contact details anywhere in it, so it stays inside Upwork's rules.

Every claim below comes from the portfolio site: 10+ years in finance & accounting, MBA,
Philippines, n8n / Zapier / Make, and the case studies (invoice approval, lead follow-up,
AI voice appointment setter).

---

## The script

**[0:00 – 0:08] Hook** *(lean in slightly, no smile yet: you're naming their problem)*

> Is your team doing the same tasks over and over, every single week?
> I can make those tasks run on their own, so your team can focus on real work.

**[0:08 – 0:20] Who you are** *(now smile)*

> Hi, I'm Ramon, an AI Automation Specialist based in the Philippines. I build automated
> workflows and AI assistants that connect the tools your business already uses, mostly in
> n8n, and also Zapier and Make.

**[0:20 – 0:33] Why you're different**

> Before automation, I spent over ten years in finance and accounting, and I have an MBA.
> So I know what an approval limit is, why every step needs a record, and what happens when
> a number is wrong. I build for that from day one.

**[0:33 – 0:55] Proof** *(slow down here: this is the part clients remember)*

> For example: an invoice system that reads invoices from email, checks them against
> purchase orders, gets approval in Slack, and adds the bill to QuickBooks.
> A lead system that replies to new leads in under a second, and alerts the sales manager
> if nobody follows up within fifteen minutes.
> And an AI voice agent that books appointments while the caller is still on the line.

**[0:55 – 1:07] How you work**

> Everything I build is tested with real data, sends an alert when something fails, and
> comes with documentation and a handover walkthrough. And it runs in your account, not mine.

**[1:07 – 1:20] Call to action** *(look straight into the lens, slow, warm)*

> If there's a process eating up your team's week, send me a message and tell me what it is.
> I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth automating, and how I'd build it.
> Talk soon.

## Recording notes (talking head only)

- **Read it in sections, not in one take.** Record each block separately and cut between
  them. Each cut is a chance to punch in slightly (crop 10–15% closer) so a single-camera
  video doesn't feel static. Punch in on the Proof and Call to action.
- **Frame:** eyes about one third from the top, a bit of space above your head, camera at
  eye level. Sit facing a window or soft light; nothing bright behind you.
- **Audio matters more than video.** A lapel mic or wired earphones' mic beats the laptop
  mic. Record in a room with soft furnishings.
- **Pace:** if a full read runs past 90 seconds, cut the Zapier/Make mention and the third
  proof example (voice agent) first.
- **Captions:** add burned-in subtitles. Many people scroll Upwork with the sound off.
- **Thumbnail/first frame:** start on you already mid-sentence or smiling, not on you
  reaching for the record button.
- **Don't memorise word for word.** Know each block's point and say it in your own words;
  a slightly imperfect natural read beats a perfect recited one.
