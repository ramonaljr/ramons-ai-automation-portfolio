## About this build

Built in n8n for **Voltara Commercial Solar, a fictional sample client** installing rooftop solar for businesses. The workflow itself is real and complete.

## The problem

- **Slow first response.** Enquiries waited in an inbox while interest cooled.
- **Uneven assignment.** Some reps were overloaded while others had nothing.
- **No context.** Reps called without knowing the building, roof or electricity use.
- **Leads forgotten.** Nobody noticed when a lead was never called back.

## What I built

1. **Receive and reply fast.** The website form sends the lead in; name and email are checked, and the visitor gets a "received" reply in under a second.
2. **Check if we know them.** Airtable is searched for existing customers and repeat enquiries from the last 24 hours.
3. **AI reads the message.** What they want, how urgent, budget hints, and any building, roof, ownership or electricity details they state. It never guesses.
4. **Score and sort by rules.** Points for electricity use, ownership, roof type and age, urgency and more, with the reasons listed. Large, small and existing customers get a rep; low-priority and duplicates are logged.
5. **Assign and save.** The rep for that lead type assigned least recently gets it, and the lead is saved with a 15-minute deadline.
6. **Reply and alert.** AI writes a short reply in the lead's own terms, sent from Gmail; the rep is tagged in Slack with the score and site details.
7. **Low-priority and logging.** Every lead, including invalid forms, goes to the lead log.
8. **15-minute follow-up check.** Every 5 minutes, leads with no rep or no follow-up are reassigned to the sales manager.
9. **Error alerts.** Failures go to Slack and an error log.

## Safeguards

- **AI never sets the score;** fixed rules do, so scores can be explained and changed without rewriting prompts.
- **No promises in replies:** no prices, savings, payback periods or system sizes before a site visit.
- **Duplicates are only logged:** no second email or Slack alert.
- **The manager is the backstop** for any lead not followed up.

## Outcomes

- Every enquiry gets an instant reply and a personal follow-up email.
- Reps receive scored, explained leads in rotation.
- No lead goes more than 15 minutes without someone responsible for it.
