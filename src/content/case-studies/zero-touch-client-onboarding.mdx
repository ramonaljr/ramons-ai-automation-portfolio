## Problem

Client intake was bogged down by fragmented manual steps: after a prospect agreed to a proposal, an account manager had to manually draft a service agreement, create custom Google Drive project directories, assign Airtable tasks, set up communication channels, and send welcome emails. This caused 24-48 hour delays in kickoff and dropped client enthusiasm.

## My Role

Architected a unified **zero-touch onboarding workflow in n8n** that activates instantly upon signed agreements or form submission.

## Key Outcomes

- **Zero-delay kickoff**: Client onboarding time dropped from 36 hours to under **45 seconds**.
- **100% data consistency**: Client records, contract values, and billing contacts automatically synced across CRM, Google Drive, and Airtable.
- **Instant team alerts**: Project team receives an interactive Telegram card with all provisioned links and contact info.

## Workflow Mechanics

1. **Trigger**: Typeform / Webhook receives signed client submission.
2. **Directory & Document Creation**: n8n calls Google Drive & Docs APIs to generate templated project folders, permission-gated deliverables sheets, and kickoff agendas.
3. **CRM & Database Sync**: Creates unified client record in Airtable with unique ID and status tracking.
4. **Notification & Calendar**: Automatically sends personalized welcome email with onboarding video, plus schedules kickoff via calendar API.
