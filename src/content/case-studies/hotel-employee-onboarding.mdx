## About this build

Built in n8n for **Casa Marindo Hotel Group, a fictional sample client**. The workflow itself is real and complete.

## The problem

- **Repeated setup** of folders, records, orientation and access for every hire.
- **Document chasing** by individual emails, with no clear view of what was still missing.
- **Forgotten access.** Key cards and system access were late because nobody followed up.
- **Repeat questions** about policy reaching HR, with the risk of inconsistent answers.

## What I built

1. **New hire form and plan.** HR fills in one form. Missing details stop the run and alert HR; otherwise a plan of tasks and documents is built for that hotel role.
2. **Set everything up.** A private Drive folder, the employee record, a Day 1 orientation in Google Calendar, a Slack message to IT and the shift manager, and a welcome email.
3. **Trackable rows.** Every task and document becomes its own Airtable row with an owner and status.
4. **Document upload.** The new hire uploads documents through a form; each is saved to Drive and marked received, which ends its reminders.
5. **Daily check at 8:00.** At most one email a day per person: milestone check-ins on day −7, 0, 3, 7 and 30, otherwise a reminder for missing documents. IT is chased for access and key cards alongside.
6. **Close onboarding.** After 30 days, with all documents in and IT confirmed, onboarding is marked complete and announced.
7. **HR assistant.** A chat assistant answers questions using only the uploaded HR policies.
8. **Error alerts.** Failures go to Slack and an error log.

## Safeguards

- **Incomplete forms stop early,** so nobody is half set up.
- **One email a day at most,** with missing documents folded into milestone emails.
- **IT is never blocked** by missing paperwork.
- **The assistant says when policy is silent** rather than guessing about pay, notice or rest breaks.

## Outcomes

- New hires are set up the same way every time, from one form.
- HR can see every outstanding task and document in Airtable.
- The first 30 days are followed up without anyone tracking them by hand.
