## About this build

Built in n8n for **TalentBridge Staffing, a fictional sample client**. The workflow itself is real and complete.

## The problem

- **Inconsistent chasing.** Some invoices got daily reminders, others none.
- **Wrong reminders.** Customers who disputed an invoice, promised a date or had already paid kept being chased.
- **Unread replies.** Replies sat in an inbox while the next reminder went out.
- **Surprised account managers** when a harsh final notice reached a key client.

## What I built

1. **Data from three places.** QuickBooks for unpaid invoices and each customer's billing email, Pipedrive for the account owner and open deals, and Postgres for reminder history, loaded once per run.
2. **Policy rules decide.** Which invoices get a reminder, and how firm, follows company policy, never AI. Disputed invoices, wrong contacts and promised dates are skipped.
3. **AI writes the email** within the approved policy: firmer the later it is, with no invented fees, accusations or legal threats. The subject line is built by rules.
4. **Send and record.** Each reminder is recorded in Postgres, where one row per invoice is enforced.
5. **One final notice.** At 30+ days late, the account owner is told in Slack and given a Pipedrive task first. Only one final notice goes out; after that the account owner takes over.
6. **AI reads replies** and pulls out promised dates, invoice numbers, dispute reasons or new contacts.
7. **Disputes stop reminders** until a person clears them, with a Pipedrive task, Slack alert and saved emails.
8. **Every other reply sets the next date:** promised payments, "already paid" checks in QuickBooks, invoice copies, wrong contacts and questions.
9. **Error alerts.** Failures go to Slack and an error log; tomorrow's run picks up without double-sending.

## Safeguards

- **A dispute stops all reminders** until a person resolves it.
- **No double sends,** enforced by the database itself.
- **Fixed rules** for timing and tone; AI only writes the words.
- **Account owners are warned** before the one final notice, then take over.

## Outcomes

- Every overdue invoice is followed up consistently, by policy.
- Every customer reply is read and acted on the same day.
- The team only handles disputes, questions and key-client final notices.
