## The problem

Fernhill Outdoor Supply is a small online store selling tents, packs, boots and camp gear. Support was one inbox, answered by hand.

- **The same questions all day.** Where is my order, can I still return this, does this boot run small.
- **Slow replies.** Answers waited until someone was free, and nothing came back at night or at weekends.
- **Refunds handled loosely.** Whoever answered decided, and there was no single record of what had been promised.

## What I built

A support agent that customers message on WhatsApp, built in n8n with the WhatsApp Business Cloud API and an OpenAI model, reading the store's own data. The same agent also runs as the chat on this page.

1. **Customer messages on WhatsApp.** Each phone number gets its own conversation, so the bot remembers the last few messages. Photos, such as a picture of a damaged item, are saved to Google Drive and the link goes into the case for the team. Voice notes and stickers get a short note asking the customer to type.
2. **The agent answers from store data.** It searches the store policies document for anything about shipping, returns or products, and looks up orders and stock in Airtable. Replies are kept to a few sentences.
3. **Refunds and handovers.** When a customer asks for money back, a replacement or a discount, the agent starts a team request instead of promising it. The workflow checks the order is real, then routes it: money requests go to Slack for approval, and upset customers or unusual questions go to the team as a handover.
4. **A person decides.** The team picks Approve or Reject in a Slack form, can add a note, and the customer is emailed the decision.
5. **Logs and a daily summary.** Every conversation is saved in Airtable, errors alert the team in Slack, and a summary of the day's cases is posted at 5 PM.

## Safeguards

- **Order number and email together.** The bot shares nothing about an order until both match the records.
- **No promises about money.** The agent is told never to say a refund is approved. A person decides, in Slack.
- **Only what the policies say.** Questions the policies document does not cover are handed to a person rather than answered from general knowledge.
- **Tested before every change.** A set of 30 customer messages, from simple order checks to messages in French and Spanish, a prompt injection and an angry customer, is run after each change and scored automatically.

## Outcomes

- Customers get answers on order status, returns, shipping and products at any hour.
- Refunds and other money decisions stay with the team, with a record of who approved what.
- The team starts each day knowing what the bot handled and what is waiting for them.
