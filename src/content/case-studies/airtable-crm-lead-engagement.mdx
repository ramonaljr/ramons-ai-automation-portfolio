## About this build

Built on Zapier for **Kestrel Digital, a fictional sample client**, a small agency selling consulting, builds and support. The workflow itself is real and complete: a classic Zap built with Paths, Delay and error handlers, running against an Airtable base.

## The problem

- **Next steps lived in people's heads.** Moving a card on the board did nothing by itself.
- **Folders came late.** New projects started without a shared place for files.
- **Follow-ups were forgotten.** Quiet leads and unanswered quotes simply went cold.
- **Uneven client emails.** Welcome and thank-you notes depended on who had time that day.

## What I built

1. **Catch the stage change.** Airtable triggers the Zap when a lead's stage changes. A filter lets it through only for the five stages with an automation, and only if that stage is not already in the lead's Handled Stages.
2. **Claim the stage.** A code step prepares the greeting and the lead owner's name. The stage is then added to Handled Stages before anything is sent, so the same change can never run twice, and Paths sends the lead down the route for its stage. A lead with no email address goes down its own path, where it is logged and skipped.
3. **Ready to Start.** A Google Drive folder is created for the client, a Social Media Content task is added in Airtable, and the folder link is saved on the lead.
4. **No Response and Quoted.** A follow-up email goes out from Gmail and is logged. Two days later the lead is looked up again, and if it is still at the same stage, a second nudge is sent and logged.
5. **Approved.** AI writes a short welcome email covering the kickoff call and the shared project folder, which is sent and logged.
6. **Paid and Closed.** AI writes a thank-you suited to the kind of work, whether consulting, a build or support, and asks for a referral or a short review.
7. **If something breaks.** Each folder and email step has its own error handler. The folder, the nudges and the welcome and thank-you emails wait 15 minutes and try once more, logging a send that worked on the second try. If the retry fails, or a first follow-up fails, an alert email names the client and stage.

## Safeguards

- **Exactly once:** a stage is claimed before it acts, so repeated or late changes back off.
- **Fresh before a nudge:** the board is checked again after the two-day wait, so a client who replied is never chased.
- **Short, steady AI emails:** the welcome stays under 150 words and the thank-you under 170, both signed by the lead's owner.
- **A full record:** every email and folder is written to the lead's Pipeline Log with a timestamp, including whether it went through on a retry.
- **Failures surface:** a step that fails twice, or a first follow-up that fails once, sends an alert email instead of failing silently.
- **No email, no guess:** a lead without an email address is logged and skipped until someone fills it in.

## Outcomes

- Every stage on the board sets off its own next step without anyone remembering it.
- Quiet leads and open quotes get a follow-up and one nudge, then no more.
- New clients get a folder, a task and a welcome, and finished clients get a thank-you.
