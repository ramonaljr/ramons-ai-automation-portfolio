## About this build

Built in n8n for **Harbourline Payroll Services, a fictional sample client**: an outsourced payroll firm prospecting growing SMEs. The workflow itself is real and complete.

## The problem

- **Manual searching.** Finding the right decision-makers meant hours of searching and copying contacts.
- **Wasted credits.** Contact data was paid for on people who were a poor fit or already contacted.
- **Generic emails.** Writing a researched, personal first email for every prospect was not realistic.
- **Messy hand-off.** Promising prospects reached the CRM late, without the research behind them.

## What I built

1. **Find new prospects.** The sales team fills in a form with job titles, locations, company size, industry and the offer. Apollo's people search finds matches without using credits.
2. **Skip known prospects.** Anyone already in the prospects sheet is skipped, whatever their status, and the rest are ranked by seniority.
3. **Enrich and score.** Only the top prospects are enriched in Apollo. Each is scored out of 100 by fixed rules (seniority, title match, verified email, company size, industry), with the reasons listed.
4. **AI research and draft.** The company's own website is read, and AI writes a one-line summary and a short first email using only facts from that website.
5. **Review queue.** Every prospect is written to a Google Sheet. Those scoring 60+ with a verified email wait for review; Slack gets one digest per run.
6. **Approved into Pipedrive.** Every 15 minutes, approved prospects are added to Pipedrive. The organisation and person are looked up first, a deal is created, and the research and draft email are pinned to it.
7. **Error alerts.** If anything fails, Slack hears which step failed, with a link to the run.

## Safeguards

- **Nothing is sent automatically.** The rep reviews and sends from Pipedrive.
- **Rules, not AI, decide the score,** so every score can be explained.
- **No duplicates** in the sheet or the CRM.
- **Grounded AI drafts:** no invented facts, prices or guarantees, and a flag when the website gave nothing useful.

## Outcomes

- One form produces a ranked, researched list of new prospects with draft emails.
- Apollo credits go to the best-fit people first.
- Approved prospects reach Pipedrive with everything the rep needs to send.
