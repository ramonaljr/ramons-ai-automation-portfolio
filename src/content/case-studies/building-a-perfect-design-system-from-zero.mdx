## Problem

A fast-growing multi-entity business was manually processing 200+ vendor invoices per week across different formats (PDFs, scanned receipts, email attachments). Accounts Payable staff spent over 25 hours weekly cross-referencing invoice line items with purchase orders, calculating tax withholdings, and manually typing General Ledger (GL) entries into their accounting software — resulting in frequent human error and delayed month-end closes.

## My Role

As an AI Automation Specialist with 10 years of accounting experience, I designed and deployed the entire automated AP pipeline in **n8n (self-hosted)**, integrating **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** for structured vision extraction, automated validation against ERP item ledgers, and seamless sync to accounting software.

## Key Outcomes

- **92% reduction** in manual invoice processing time (from 25 hours/week to under 2 hours).
- **Sub-30-second processing time** per invoice from email receipt to GL draft.
- **Zero data-entry discrepancies** on standard recurring vendor billings.
- **Automated Human-in-the-Loop review** for any variances exceeding 2% or $1,000 threshold.

## How I Solved It

### 1. Process Mapping & Trigger Architecture
Before writing a single node, I mapped out every step of the vendor lifecycle:
- Automated ingestion trigger watching dedicated Gmail and Google Drive intake folders via webhooks.
- Multi-format file routing (PDF, PNG, TIFF) handling multi-page invoices and email body context.

> Leveraging my accounting background, I knew that data extraction is useless without accounting validation: matching vendor TINs, checking payment terms (Net 30/60), and calculating withholding taxes.

### 2. Multi-Modal Vision Extraction with Claude
I built custom prompt engineering chains in n8n utilizing Claude's vision capabilities:
- Enforces strict **JSON schema output** containing vendor name, invoice date, due date, line-item breakdown, tax rates, and total amounts.
- Includes fallback logic and confidence scoring for low-resolution or hand-signed physical vouchers.

### 3. Business Rule Validation & Human-in-the-Loop
- Reconciles extracted line items against active Purchase Orders in Airtable/Database.
- Automatic routing: If confidence is $>98\%$ and variance is $0$, draft GL entries are pushed to QuickBooks/ERP.
- If an unknown vendor or price discrepancy is flagged, an interactive Telegram/Slack card is sent to the financial manager with "Approve" or "Edit" buttons.

## System Resilience & Documentation

Every node includes **try/catch error-handling branches** that log failed executions and ping admins with exact error diagnostics. Complete video walkthroughs and markdown SOPs were handed over so non-technical finance staff can add new vendor rules independently.
