## About this build

Built in n8n for **Braewick Construction Group, a fictional sample client**. The workflow itself is real and complete.

## The problem

- **Manual matching.** Each invoice was compared by hand with the purchase order and delivery record.
- **Duplicate payments.** The same invoice re-sent in a new email could be paid twice.
- **Progress payment risk.** Paying more than the contract value, skipping retention or paying without a lien waiver.
- **Slow approvals** and no single record of what was posted, rejected or held.

## What I built

1. **Receive the invoice.** Every 5 minutes, Gmail is checked for unread emails with a PDF. Scanned images with no readable text go to the team instead of the AI.
2. **Skip duplicates.** Each email and file is checked against the invoice log before any AI cost, and again by vendor and invoice number after reading.
3. **AI reads the invoice.** Vendor, PO and job numbers, cost code, invoice type, billing to date, amounts already approved, retention and lien waiver, never invented.
4. **Compare with records.** If the AI is unsure, a person checks the details in Slack. The invoice is then compared with the purchase order and delivery record in Airtable.
5. **Approval in Slack.** Invoices with issues go to accounts payable; clean ones to the budget owner. The workflow waits up to 2 days for an answer.
6. **Add to QuickBooks and log.** The vendor is found in QuickBooks, the bill is added at the amount to pay after retention, and every outcome is logged. The email is marked as read.
7. **Error alerts.** Failures go to Slack and an error log, with the invoice flagged for manual entry.

## Safeguards

- **Two duplicate checks,** before and after the AI reads the invoice.
- **Fixed rules check the numbers:** amounts within 2% of the PO and delivery record, billing that adds up, retention held, lien waiver attached.
- **A person approves every bill** before it reaches QuickBooks.
- **Missing vendors are never guessed:** the bill is held and the team alerted.

## Outcomes

- Every invoice is matched against its PO and delivery record automatically.
- Approvers see the issues already flagged and decide in Slack.
- QuickBooks only receives approved bills, at the right amount, with a full log behind them.
