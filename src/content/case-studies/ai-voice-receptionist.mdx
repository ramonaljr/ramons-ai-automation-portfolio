## Problem

A busy professional service firm was missing 40% of inbound after-hours and weekend phone inquiries. Hiring a dedicated 24/7 call center was cost-prohibitive, and basic voicemail resulted in an 85% drop-off rate as callers moved on to competitors.

## My Role

Designed and implemented an autonomous **AI Voice Receptionist** using **VAPI and Retell AI**, powered by **ElevenLabs natural voice synthesis** and orchestrated through **n8n** to handle conversational inquiries, answer FAQs, and book calendar appointments in real-time.

## Key Outcomes

- **100% call capture rate**: Handled after-hours and peak overflow calls with zero wait time.
- **Sub-600ms latency**: Natural, human-like voice conversations with intelligent interrupt handling.
- **3.2x increase in booked consultations** compared to traditional voicemail callbacks.

## Technical Architecture

1. **Telephony & Voice Pipeline**: Twilio SIP trunk routed directly to VAPI / Retell AI with ElevenLabs custom voice cloning.
2. **Tool-Calling Functions in n8n**: During the call, the AI agent executes live tools (e.g. `check_calendar_availability`, `book_appointment`, `lookup_service_pricing`).
3. **CRM Integration & SMS Confirmation**: As soon as a slot is selected, n8n sends immediate SMS confirmation with calendar invite and updates the client record in Airtable.
