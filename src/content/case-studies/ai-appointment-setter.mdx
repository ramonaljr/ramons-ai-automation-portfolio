## The problem

Appointments came in by phone, and the phone was not always answered.

- **Missed calls.** Calls after hours or during busy periods went unanswered, and most callers hung up without leaving a message.
- **Slow booking.** Answered calls meant reading out free times and typing the booking into the calendar by hand.
- **Rescheduling and cancelling.** Every change meant finding the original booking, editing the calendar and updating the records, and the two often drifted apart.
- **No record of calls.** There was no consistent log of who called and what happened.

## What I built

A VAPI voice agent that holds the conversation, and five n8n flows it uses during the call to act on the real calendar and records.

1. **Check availability.** Looks up the requested time in Google Calendar. If it is taken, it returns the free time ranges so the agent can offer alternatives.
2. **Book.** Checks the caller has given everything needed, converts the time to the business's time zone, creates the Google Calendar event and logs the booking in Airtable.
3. **Reschedule.** Finds the original appointment in Airtable, moves the calendar event and updates the record.
4. **Cancel.** Finds the appointment, deletes the calendar event and marks the record as cancelled.
5. **Call results.** Saves the outcome of every call in Airtable, creating or updating the caller's record.

## Safeguards

- **Missing details caught.** Each workflow checks the details first. If something is missing, the agent is told what to ask for instead of guessing.
- **Confirmed free before booking.** A time is only booked once the calendar shows it is free.
- **Friendly errors.** If a booking fails, the agent gets a plain message to pass on, so the caller always knows what happened.
- **Calendar and records together.** Bookings, changes and cancellations update Google Calendar and Airtable in the same run.

## Outcomes

- Callers can book, reschedule or cancel at any hour without waiting for a person.
- The calendar and Airtable stay in step, because every change is made to both.
- Every call leaves a record, so the team can see who called and what was agreed.
