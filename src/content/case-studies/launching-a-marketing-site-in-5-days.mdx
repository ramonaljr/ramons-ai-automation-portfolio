## Problem

Tracking portfolio earnings across dozen publicly traded companies required repetitive manual reading of 100+ page 10-Q/10-K filings, earnings call transcripts, and press releases every quarter. Analysts spent 15+ hours per company manually extracting revenue segments, EBITDA margins, and forward capex guidance to update Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model assumptions.

## My Role

Combining my MBA valuation expertise with n8n automation, I built an end-to-end autonomous research workflow that scrapes new filings as they drop on SEC EDGAR, runs structured LLM extraction chains, and populates updated model assumption tables.

## Key Outcomes

- **80% faster quarterly updates** per covered company (from 8 hours down to ~90 minutes).
- **Automated sentiment & guidance variance detection** comparing management tone quarter-over-quarter.
- **Instant executive briefings** generated automatically and delivered to email/Telegram before market open.

## How I Solved It

### 1. Document Ingestion & Chunking
- Created scheduled n8n workflows monitoring RSS feeds and financial API endpoints.
- Auto-downloads PDF transcripts and normalizes tables into structured markdown.

### 2. Multi-Pass Financial Extraction
- **Pass 1 (KPI Extraction)**: Claude extracts organic revenue growth, operating margin guidance, and capital expenditures.
- **Pass 2 (Guidance Revision)**: Compares current guidance against prior quarter baseline, highlighting upward/downward revisions.
- **Pass 3 (DCF Assumption Update)**: Translates qualitative management remarks into numeric WACC and terminal growth sensitivities.

### 3. Output Delivery & Reporting
Clean data tables are automatically synced to Airtable workbooks with downloadable executive summaries ready for client presentation.
