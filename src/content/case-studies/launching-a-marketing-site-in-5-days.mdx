## Problem

A distributed team was losing 12+ hours per employee each week searching for critical operational procedures, client contracts, and technical SOPs scattered across multiple Google Drive folders, PDFs, and internal databases. Traditional keyword search frequently missed relevant context or returned outdated document versions.

## My Role

Designed and deployed an automated **RAG Knowledge Base & Document Intelligence System** in **n8n**, connecting document storage directly to **Pinecone vector embeddings** and **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** to provide accurate, context-aware answers with direct citations.

## Key Outcomes

- **95% reduction in search time**: Team members retrieve verified answers in $< 2$ seconds.
- **Zero document hallucinations**: Strict prompt constraints require the agent to cite source paragraphs and disclaim unknown queries.
- **Automated re-indexing**: n8n monitors Google Drive folders via webhooks and automatically vectorizes updated files.

## Technical Architecture

### 1. Automated Document Ingestion & Chunking
- Webhook trigger detects new or modified PDFs and Docs in Google Drive.
- Text is cleaned, normalized, and split into recursive chunks with contextual metadata (document title, author, update timestamp).

### 2. Vector Embedding & Hybrid Search
- Text chunks are embedded using OpenAI / Voyage embeddings and stored in **Pinecone & Supabase Vector**.
- Uses hybrid keyword-vector retrieval to balance semantic intent with exact term matching.

### 3. Multi-Channel Assistant Interface
- Connected to **Telegram and Slack bots**, allowing team members to ask questions in natural language and receive formatted answers with source links directly in their daily workspace.
